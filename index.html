Capabilities

Everything you need to stay organized, nothing you have to pay for.

01

Drag & drop

Move tasks between lists and reorder boards with mouse or touch.

02

Subtasks

Break big work into smaller pieces. Indent and nest subtasks under any task.

03

Due dates & repeats

Star what matters, set due dates, and schedule daily, weekly, or monthly repeats.

04

Attachments

Attach files or link URLs to any task. Everything stays with the task it belongs to.

05

Custom themes

Light, dark, midnight, forest, or cyberpunk, plus per-board background images.

06

Import & export

Bring in Google Tasks JSON or bulk-create. Soft-delete keeps everything recoverable.

How it works

Three levels of organization, three minutes to set up.

1

Create a board

One board per project or area of life: Work, Home, Trip Planning. Give it a name and a background.

2

Add lists

Organize work into columns: To Do, In Progress, Done. Rename, recolor, and reorder them freely.

3

Forge your tasks

Drop tasks in, drag them across lists as you make progress, star priorities, and check things off.

Built on open foundations

The stack is boring on purpose. That is the feature.

ST

Modern stack

Node.js, Express, and MongoDB on the backend. Vanilla ES modules and Vite on the frontend. No heavy frameworks to babysit.

SEC

Secure by default

JWT auth with rotating refresh tokens, password hashing, rate limiting, and strict per-user data isolation.

DB

Your database

Point it at any MongoDB: Atlas, a VPS, or your own cluster. Backups and migrations stay under your control.
